Roslyn packs a lot of elevation change into a small village. The ground drops from a high point of about 253 feet near Piper Court to Hempstead Harbor at sea level, and every season sends water along that slope in a different way. What follows is organized by season and terrain, because that is how water behaves here: freeze and thaw in winter, runoff in storms, and harbor-side moisture all year. Roslyn is a village of about 2,988 people, so it is small, but its geography makes jobs vary a lot from one address to the next.

Winter on the moraine: freezing pipes and thaw leaks

Roslyn sits on the Harbor Hill Moraine on Long Island's North Shore, and winters bring freezing weather. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages and crawl spaces are the usual victims. A split pipe often stays quiet while frozen and then floods a wall cavity the day it thaws.

If a faucet slows to a trickle during a cold snap, open cabinet doors under sinks and let the tap drip. Our frozen pipe water damage page covers what to do once a line has already split.

From 253 feet down to sea level

The village elevation is 98 feet, but the range is large. The highest point, near Piper Court, is about 253 feet, and Hempstead Harbor sits at sea level. Harbor Hill, the highest point in Nassau County, lies mostly in East Hills, with its western slope partly in Roslyn.

On a slope, storm runoff travels downhill and collects where the ground flattens. Homes at the bottom of a grade, or below a neighbor's driveway, are the ones that see water at foundation walls and window wells. Ask your own street's grading before you decide whether a basement problem is plumbing or runoff.

Roslyn Landing, the creek and the grist mill

The waterfront promenade at Roslyn Landing runs beside Hempstead Harbor, and Roslyn Creek follows the east side of the promenade. The 18th century Roslyn Grist Mill, a Dutch-framed watermill now under multi-phase restoration, is a reminder that this village grew around moving water. Buildings close to the creek and harbor deal with moist air and wet ground as a matter of course.

That means mold risk after a loss is real near the water. Dry quickly, keep materials ventilated, and if mold is found, New York State requires licensed mold assessors and remediators. The same person cannot do both on one property. See mold remediation for the process.

Emergency steps while water keeps spreading in Roslyn

Before we arrive, do what you can safely do. These cards go in order of urgency.

Shut off the source

Close the valve at the fixture, or the main if needed. If the water is coming in from outside, do not go outdoors into moving water.

Panel check

Shutting off breakers for the wet rooms is fine only when you can reach the panel without stepping in water. Otherwise leave it and contact the utility.

Raise belongings

Get valuables, paperwork and electronics above the waterline. Move furniture onto blocks.

Avoid the water

Floodwater and sewage can be contaminated. Keep family and pets out and do not touch standing water barehanded.

Photograph and call

Shoot the waterline and damaged items, then use the 24/7 line.

Flood and storm damage in Roslyn

Roslyn Landing sits right on Hempstead Harbor, and storm surge or a cloudburst can push water toward low ground there. Anyone near the promenade or Roslyn Creek should think about that before the next forecast.

For anything involving rising water, start with flood damage restoration. For wind, gutter failures and roof leaks, storm water damage cleanup covers the sequence. Find your address on the FEMA flood map and confirm with your insurer whether any flood coverage exists.

Cost factors and your insurance policy

Scope and price come first, before work starts. These items affect the total:

  • The water category, from clean supply water to contaminated flood or sewage water.
  • The affected area, including walls, floors and any crawl spaces.
  • The materials, since plaster, drywall, hardwood and carpet each respond differently.
  • Drying days and how much equipment they require.
  • The rebuild work after drying.
  • Insurance: standard homeowners policies often exclude flood, so check your policy for a back-up endorsement or a separate flood policy.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Roslyn?

Yes. Roslyn's own ZIP is 11576, and 11577 also serves Roslyn Heights, with part of it in the village. We cover both, from Roslyn Landing on Hempstead Harbor up the slope toward Piper Court.

Is emergency water removal available in Roslyn?

Yes. The 24/7 line is staffed every day, and crews travel from Port Washington.

Does homeowners insurance cover flood damage in Roslyn?

Usually not. A typical policy excludes flood, and coverage comes from a separate flood policy through NFIP or a private carrier, or a water back-up endorsement. Ask your insurer.

Can homes near the harbor or creek get mold after water damage?

Mold needs moisture, so wet materials near the water should be dried quickly. For health questions, see a physician, and use a licensed mold assessor and remediator.

What should I do if a pipe freezes?

Shut the main, open the faucet, and call us if the pipe has split. Never use open flame to thaw it.

Who is my water supplier in Roslyn?

Check your bill. The Roslyn Water District serves nearby areas, but suppliers vary by address.
