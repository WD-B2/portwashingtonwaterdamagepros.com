Emergency Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Port Washington, NY

A failing tank can release dozens of gallons, so shutting the cold inlet and power comes before anything else.

☎ Call (516) 845-9582

After a tank or its fittings leak, cleanup means pulling out the water and drying the floors and walls it reached. If yours is leaking now, call the 24/7 line. While you wait, close the cold water inlet on top of the tank, shut off the power or gas as shown below, and move boxes and stored items off the wet floor.

A tank holds a large volume, and a split seam lets it all out. A leak at a fitting or valve is slower but can still soak a utility room for days. Either way, the water has to be removed and the surrounding floor and walls dried.

Shutoff steps for gas and electric heaters

The water inlet is the same for both. The energy source is different.

StepGas heaterElectric heater
1. WaterClose the cold inlet valve on topClose the cold inlet valve on top
2. EnergyTurn the gas control to Off or PilotFor an electric unit, flip the breaker only if the panel is dry
3. Smell checkGas odor: get everyone out, then call the utility or 911If you see sparks or wet wiring, leave and call the utility
4. DrainAttach a hose to the drain valve only after power or gas is offSame, and do not touch the element while wet
5. ReliefOpen a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressureOpen a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure

A water heater is leaking right now

Quick actions that limit the loss before the crew arrives.

  • Close the cold water inlet valve on top of the tank to stop new water entering.
  • Turn off gas or power as the table above describes, and stay out if the floor is wet near outlets.
  • Move boxes, tools and stored items off the floor of the utility room.
  • Do not open the drain valve if the water is hot, because it can scald.
  • Take photos of the tank, the label and the water line on the wall.
  • Leave the heater in place so the plumber and the insurer can see the failure point.

Gas, power and hot water

Safety first

A gas heater that smells of gas is an emergency. If gas is in the air, everyone goes outside and the first calls are to the utility and 911. Never stand in water to reach a panel. Water from the tank can be hot enough to scald, so keep children and pets away from the area.

Why tanks fail, and what hard water has to do with it

Tanks wear from the inside. Sediment settles at the bottom, scale builds on elements and the anode rod is consumed over time. The Port Washington Water District reported total hardness of 173 mg/L in its April 7, 2025 sample, and hard water is a known contributor to scale on tanks and fittings.

That does not mean every tank here will fail early. It does mean flushing the tank, checking the relief valve and exercising shutoff valves are worth doing. A tank that is old, rusted or wet at the base should be replaced before it fails.

What a slow leak looks like

A slow leak causes damage because no one notices it.

Rust streaks

Orange stains on the tank or the fittings point to a seep.

A damp drain pan

If the pan under the tank holds water, something is dripping.

A musty smell

A smell in the utility room often means moisture in the floor.

Warped baseboard

Swelling at the bottom of the wall near the heater shows wicking.

A rising water bill

If the water meter keeps turning while every fixture is off, a hidden leak is likely.

What gets wet around a water heater

Basement floors take the most water, and concrete dries slowly. Finished walls next to the heater wick water upward from the baseboard, and carpet or stored boxes soak it up. Drain pans catch small leaks, but a tank failure overflows them quickly.

Water extraction comes first, as covered on the extraction page, and the basement page explains finished and unfinished spaces. Affected walls are checked using the methods on the wall and drywall page.

Repair or replace the heater

That choice belongs to a licensed plumber. These are the honest trade-offs.

Replace the tank

A leaking tank body cannot be fixed. Replacement is the only option.

Replace a valve or fitting

Cheap if the tank is sound, but check the age before spending.

Add a drain pan and leak sensor

A pan with a drain line and a sensor limits the next leak.

Consider a tankless unit

Removes the stored volume, with a higher install cost and a different venting setup.

Cost factors for heater leak cleanup

Four or five things set the price. The water category is usually clean. The area covers the floor and any wall that wicked. Materials such as concrete, finished drywall and carpet dry differently. How long the leak ran sets the equipment and the drying days. Rebuild scope covers drywall and flooring replaced after drying.

The scope and price get explained first, before any gear is set up. Phone the 24/7 line and tell dispatch about the tank and which room it sits in.

Insurance and water heaters

Insurers often view a tank that ruptures at once as sudden and accidental damage, though the tank itself usually is not covered. A leak that went on for a long time may be excluded. Your policy wording decides, so read it and call the insurer.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle water heater leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Port Washington?

Yes. Dispatch is staffed day and night, with same-day response depending on crew availability. Close the cold inlet and turn off power or gas before you call.

How do I shut off a leaking water heater?

Close the cold inlet valve on top. For gas, set the control to Off. For an electric heater, go to the breaker only when the floor around the panel is dry.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired?

A leaking tank cannot. A leaking valve or fitting can, if the tank is sound.

Is my water heater leak covered by insurance?

The resulting damage may be, but the tank usually is not. Ask your insurer.

Does hard water cause water heater leaks?

Hard water is a known contributor to scale, which can shorten the life of a tank. It is not the only cause.

Should I drain the tank myself?

Not if the water is hot or the floor is wet near power. Wait for a plumber.
